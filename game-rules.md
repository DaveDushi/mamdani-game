# Mamdani Run game rules

Run through three lanes of New York traffic. Painted arrows mark a route. The food stamp sits at the gate, above a jump or under a beam. Colored stamps flank a rare power-up. Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump, or down to slide. The arrow keys and WASD also work. Space jumps. Tap pause, press P or press Escape to pause. Resume gives you a three-second countdown with the street and all effect timers frozen.

| Encounter | How to play it |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow taxi | Jump or change lanes. |
| Protester | Change lanes. |
| Scaffold or halal cart | Slide underneath or change lanes. |
| Low striped barrier / overhead striped beam | Follow ↑ with a jump or ↓ with a slide. On a two-route gate, pick either marked lane. |
| Bottle | Avoid it. “Haram!” reverses left/right and jump/slide for five seconds. |
| Food stamp | Collect the ticket in the marked lane. The wallet receives your after-tax share when you finish. |
| Mask | Clears traffic for three seconds. |
| Keffiyeh | Protects you from traffic collisions for five seconds. |
| Rainbow | Grants seven seconds of invincibility, blocks bottle confusion, and attracts nearby stamps. |

Jump over a cab or striped barrier, slide under cover, or narrowly dodge traffic to earn a clean move. Chain three with less than five seconds between moves for one extra stamp.

A first unprotected collision brings Trump into pursuit. Stay clear for five seconds to shake him. A second hit during the chase ends the run. Each 100 stamps collected triggers a 90-stamp audit. At the finish, City Hall settles the remainder at the same rate. Partial progress toward the next banked stamp carries across completed runs.

Bank 150 stamps across completed runs to unlock Night Shift, equivalent to 1,500 pickups and stunt bonuses in a new wallet. Day Run opens with lane changes, introduces compulsory jumps and slides and accelerates over several kilometres. Night Shift starts faster on wet streets, offers fewer power-ups and keeps its own best distance on the device. Day Run posts distance to the leaderboard. Your wallet keeps growing in both modes.

The runner wears each active pickup until its timer expires. Your best distance and selected outfit stay on the device. All five looks are available from the Outfits screen and have the same gameplay abilities.

## Family races

Each run starts a fresh series. The street pauses at a clear stretch after each checkpoint for a head-to-head 100 m sprint. Wait for the starting signal, then tap the sprint pad or press Space repeatedly. Holding the key counts once. A loss ends the run and settles the stamp receipt. A win saves the rival you beat and returns you to the street after a countdown. Sprint metres do not add to the street leaderboard score.

Shop & outfits sells five permanent race-speed upgrades using your spendable after-tax stamps. Training helps on the sprint track. Lifetime earnings still count toward Night Shift when you spend them. Beat Donald and continue in Overtime at 110% of the usual street pace.

Passing protest signs and street posters get a large-print caption. Pause to reread the last one, or open Posters from the menu to browse all nine prints.
