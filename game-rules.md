# Mamdani Run game rules

Run through three lanes of New York traffic. A trail of three gold stamps marks a gap. Color-matched stamps lead to a power-up. Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump, or down to slide. The arrow keys and WASD also work. Space jumps. Tap pause, press P or press Escape to pause. Resume gives you a three-second countdown with the street and all effect timers frozen.

| Encounter | How to play it |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow taxi | Jump or change lanes. |
| Protester | Change lanes. |
| Scaffold or halal cart | Slide underneath or change lanes. |
| Bottle | Avoid it. “Haram!” reverses left/right and jump/slide for five seconds. |
| Food stamp | Collect it to add one stamp to your wallet. |
| Mask | Clears traffic for three seconds. |
| Keffiyeh | Protects you from traffic collisions for five seconds. |
| Rainbow | Grants seven seconds of invincibility, blocks bottle confusion, and attracts nearby stamps. |

Jump over a cab, slide under a cart or scaffold, or narrowly dodge traffic to earn a clean move. Chain three with less than five seconds between moves for three extra stamps.

A first unprotected collision brings Trump into pursuit. Stay clear for five seconds to shake him. A second hit during the chase ends the run. At the finish, City Hall taxes half your stamps, rounded down. The rest goes into your device's stamp wallet.

Bank 30 stamps across completed runs to unlock Night Shift. Day Run accelerates over several kilometres and posts distance to the existing leaderboard. Night Shift starts faster on wet streets, offers fewer power-ups and keeps its own best distance on the device. Your wallet keeps growing in both modes.

The runner wears each active pickup until its timer expires. Your best distance and selected outfit stay on the device. All five looks are available from the Outfits screen and have the same gameplay abilities.

Passing protest signs and street posters get a large-print caption. Pause to reread the last one, or open Posters from the menu to browse all nine prints.
