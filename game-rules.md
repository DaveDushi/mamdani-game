# Mamdani Run game rules

Run through three lanes of New York traffic. Short white dashes mark the open lanes. The food stamp sits at the gate, above a jump or under a beam. Pick up a mask, keffiyeh, rainbow, or miniature bus to activate its power. Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump, or down to slide. The arrow keys and WASD also work. Space jumps. Tap pause, press P or press Escape to pause. Resume gives you a three-second countdown with the street and all effect timers frozen. Restart and Menu drop the run without banking its stamps.

## Score

Your score is points: street metres plus style. Jump over a cab or barrier, slide under cover, or narrowly dodge traffic to make a clean move. A clean move pays 10 points times the multiplier. A stamp pays 5 points times the multiplier. A family win pays 500 points.

The multiplier runs from x1 to x5. Each third clean move in a chain, with less than five seconds between moves, raises it one level and pays one extra stamp. Five seconds without a clean move costs a level. A hit resets it to x1. Its clock stops during races, pauses and bus and bike rides, and while a power-up shields you.

After the opening streets, a 78° Heatwave gives you 16 seconds of double style, and each three-move streak pays five stamps. Its clock pauses for rides and family sprints. The Stamp Office includes the bonus in your taxable earnings.

## Trump

Trump starts 24 m behind you. The meter shows your lead, up to 40 m. A hit costs 18 m. A hit with a lead of 18 m or less ends the run. A head-on wall hit ends an unprotected run whatever the lead.

A clean move adds 1.5 m and a stamp adds 0.3 m. Below 20 m you pull away by about 1.6 m each second. Above 20 m the lead slips by about 0.4 m each second. After eight seconds without a clean move or a stamp he closes in, though never nearer than 10 m. He cannot catch you by running. Only a hit can. Hits in the first ten seconds of your first run never end it.

## The street

The opening gates teach one move at a time. Farther down the street, look past the first obstacle. You might duck under a scaffold, jump a cab in the same lane, then slide one lane over. These sequences get longer and the gaps get shorter. Night Shift reaches them sooner.

The street also moves through stretches: Protest March, Construction Season, Rush Hour, Street Fair, Carriage Row, Rent Freeze and Collection Day. Each has its own traffic and returns harder. All seven come up once before any repeats.

At a split, a single stamp marks the open lane. Two stamps over a taxi or under a cart or scaffold mark the stunt route. Either route leaves time to reach the next obstacle.

## Stamps, Night Shift and the shop

City Hall keeps 90 of every 100 stamps. Each 100 collected triggers an audit. At the finish, the remainder settles at the same rate. Partial progress toward the next banked stamp carries across completed runs.

Bank 100 stamps across completed runs to unlock Night Shift, equivalent to 1,000 pickups and bonuses in a new wallet. Lifetime earnings count, so spending never sets you back. Day Run opens with lane changes, introduces compulsory jumps and slides and accelerates over several kilometres. Night Shift starts faster on wet streets and offers fewer power-ups. Each mode keeps its own best score on the device. Both post to the same global leaderboard and earn stamps for the same wallet.

The shop sells permanent upgrades for after-tax stamps. Each power-up has three levels, at 4, 10 and 20 stamps, and each level adds 20% of its starting time. Sprint training has five levels, at 3, 8, 16, 28 and 45 stamps. The runner wears each active pickup until its timer expires. Mayor, Zahra Mamdani, Antifa, Imam and Communist are free outfits with the same gameplay abilities.

## Family races

Each run starts with Donald Trump Jr., then Ivanka, Eric, Tiffany and Barron. All five race offline. They wait at 1, 3.5, 7.5, 12.5 and 18.5 km, and run 64, 72, 80, 88 and 98 m. These marks never move. Beating them pays 5, 8, 12, 16 and 20 gross stamps. Open Race schedule under Sprint training in the Shop to see them with each rival’s trick.

The street pauses for each sprint when the course is clear. Tap anywhere, or press Space or Up, as fast as you can. Holding the key counts once. You slow down between taps. Against Tiffany, Barron and Donald, the Left and Right arrows or A and D hit the side pads.

| Rival | Trick |
| --- | --- |
| Donald Trump Jr. | None. Pure speed. |
| Ivanka | The pad turns gold twice. Taps then count half again. |
| Eric | The pad turns red. Hands off, or a tap trips you. |
| Tiffany | The pad jumps left, middle and right. Only the lit one counts. |
| Barron | Alternate left and right. The same foot twice goes nowhere. |
| Donald | Red lights, then alternating feet, then a gold kick. He jumps the gun, stalls, then surges. |

A loss ends the run and settles the stamp receipt. A win saves the rival you beat and returns you to the street after a countdown. Sprint metres do not add to your score.

Each training level makes every tap carry 11% more speed, up to 55%. All levels share a top speed of 13.2 m/s.

To qualify for Donald, beat all five kids in the same run, then pass both 12,000 points and the live record. If another runner raises the record first, keep running until you pass it. The run must be ranked, which takes online play and a connection at the start, and the check itself needs one too. Donald races 100 m for 50 gross stamps.

Each victory gives City Hall another sixth of the city. Buildings grow more uniform and the sky darkens. Beat Donald for the 100% takeover win, then choose Run Overtime for endless play at 110% of the usual pace. A fresh run restores the original city. Your best family result stays saved and shows as a skyline on the menu.

Passing protest signs and street posters get a large-print caption. Pause to reread the last one, or open Street posters in City Wire to browse all nine prints.
