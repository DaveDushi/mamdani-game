# Mamdani Run game rules

Run through three lanes of New York traffic. Short white dashes mark the open lanes. The food stamp sits at the gate, above a jump or under a beam. Colored stamps flank a rare power-up. Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump, or down to slide. The arrow keys and WASD also work. Space jumps. Tap pause, press P or press Escape to pause. Resume gives you a three-second countdown with the street and all effect timers frozen.

| Freeze the Rent ice | Jump the blue patch or switch to a dry lane. Grounded contact locks steering for half a second. You can still jump or slide. |
| Central Park bike dock | From the left lane, move left again at the open dock. Steer through the park barriers for one gross stamp per opening cleared. You can hop a low barrier. The path rejoins Fifth Avenue. |
| Bottle | Avoid it. “Haram!” reverses left/right and jump/slide for five seconds. |
| Food stamp | Collect the ticket in the marked lane. The wallet receives your after-tax share when you finish. |
| Mask | Clears traffic for three seconds. |
| Keffiyeh | Protects you from traffic collisions for five seconds. |
| Rainbow | Grants seven seconds of invincibility, blocks bottle confusion, and attracts nearby stamps. |
| Fast & Free bus | Steer for ten seconds. Each vehicle or worksite obstacle you smash earns one gross stamp, up to three per ride. Pass pedestrians and horses safely. |

Jump over a cab or striped barrier, slide under cover, or narrowly dodge traffic to earn a clean move. Chain three with less than five seconds between moves for one extra stamp.

After the opening streets, a 78° Heatwave gives you 16 seconds to earn two stamps for each three-move streak. Its clock pauses for rides and family sprints. The Stamp Office includes the bonus in your taxable earnings.

The opening gates teach one move at a time. Moving obstacles appear after the opening streets. Later, a dolly or boom can lead straight into another lane change, jump or slide. The gaps vary, and Night Shift reaches those combinations sooner.

A head-on wall hit ends an unprotected run immediately. Other traffic hits bring Trump into pursuit. Stay clear for five seconds to shake him. A second hit during the chase ends the run. Each 100 stamps collected triggers a 90-stamp audit. At the finish, City Hall settles the remainder at the same rate. Partial progress toward the next banked stamp carries across completed runs.

Bank 150 stamps across completed runs to unlock Night Shift, equivalent to 1,500 pickups and stunt bonuses in a new wallet. Day Run opens with lane changes, introduces compulsory jumps and slides and accelerates over several kilometres. Night Shift starts faster on wet streets and offers fewer power-ups. Each mode keeps its own best distance on the device. Both post street distances to the same Season 2.3.1 rankings and earn stamps for the same wallet.

The runner wears each active pickup until its timer expires. Your best distance and selected outfit stay on the device. Mayor, Zahra Mamdani, Antifa, Imam and Communist are free. Each has the same gameplay abilities.

## Family races

Each run starts with Donald Trump Jr., then Ivanka, Eric, Tiffany and Barron. All five race offline. Their opening street marks are 650, 1,670, 2,975, 4,650 and 6,650 m. Their heats are 64, 72, 80, 88 and 98 m respectively. A season record above 9 km moves those marks farther out and lengthens the heats, up to 100 m. Open Race schedule in the Shop for the live distances.

The street pauses for each sprint when the course is clear. On the starting signal, tap the sprint pad or press Space repeatedly. Holding the key counts once. A loss ends the run and settles the stamp receipt. A win saves the rival you beat and returns you to the street after a countdown. Only street metres count toward your ranking.

To qualify for Donald, beat all five kids in the same run, then go beyond both 9,000 m and the live season record. The earliest final starts at 9,001 street metres. If another runner raises the record first, keep running until you pass it. The check requires a connection. Donald always races 100 m.

The shop sells five permanent sprint upgrades using after-tax stamps. Each level adds 11% to the speed gained per tap. All five training levels share a top speed, and you slow between taps. Keep tapping through the finish. Lifetime earnings still count toward Night Shift when you spend them.

Each victory gives City Hall another sixth of the city. Buildings grow more uniform and the sky darkens. Beat Donald for the 100% takeover win, then choose Run Overtime for endless play at 110% of the usual pace. A fresh run restores the original city. Your best family result stays saved.

Passing protest signs and street posters get a large-print caption. Pause to reread the last one, or open Posters from the menu to browse all nine prints.
