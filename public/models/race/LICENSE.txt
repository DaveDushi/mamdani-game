The five race models use geometry and skinning from Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox) and the Avatar Studio conversions by Rohit Biradar (https://github.com/memoriesrohit/avatar-studio). Source characters are Ryan, Hannah, Luca, Olivia and Ethan. The race uses custom face and costume art and the game's run animation.
