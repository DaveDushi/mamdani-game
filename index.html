Full rules

Street. Follow the painted lane. The first gates have labels. When a delivery dolly crosses, take the lane it leaves open. Jump a low signal boom or duck a high one. Jump the blue ice patch or take a dry lane. On ice, steering locks for half a second.

A bell and a road marking announce a horse-drawn carriage. Leave its lane. At a protest, duck the police tape, then jump the fallen placard. Later pairs can switch lanes. Bottles reverse your controls for five seconds (“Haram!”).

Stretches. The street changes character as you run: Protest March, Construction Season, Rush Hour, Street Fair, Carriage Row, Rent Freeze and Collection Day. Each returns harder.

Trump. He starts 24 m back. A hit costs 18 m of lead, and a hit with 18 m or less ends the run. Below 20 m you slowly pull away. Above 20 m the lead drifts down. Eight seconds without a clean move or a stamp lets him close in, though never past 10 m. Only a hit can catch you. The first ten seconds of your first run are safe.

Score. Each metre is a point. A clean move pays 10 points and a stamp 5, times the multiplier. A family win pays 500. Every third chained clean move also pays a bonus stamp. A 16-second 78° Heatwave doubles style and pays 5 stamps per streak.

Power-ups. The keffiyeh lasts 5 seconds, the rainbow 7, the mask 3 and the Fast & Free bus 10. Smashing obstacles with the bus pays up to 8 stamps a ride. Each shop level adds 20% to the clock, up to three levels.

Central Park. At the open dock, move left again from the left lane to take a bike. Each gap you steer through pays 3 stamps. You can hop a low barrier.

Family races. Junior, Ivanka, Eric, Tiffany and Barron wait at 1, 3.5, 7.5, 12.5 and 18.5 km. They pay 5, 8, 12, 16 and 20 stamps. Each has a different trick. A loss ends the run.

Donald’s final. Beat all five in one run, then pass 12,000 points or the live record, whichever is higher. Your run needs a connection from the start. He races 100 m for 50 stamps. Beat him for 100% takeover and endless Overtime at 10% higher speed.

Online play and data

Online play posts your scores to the public leaderboard and qualifies you for Donald’s final.

The game sends a private random key with your run mode and points. The server keeps a hash of the key and gives each run a ticket, so scores and race claims can be checked. The leaderboard shows neither. A nickname is optional.

Deleting removes your nickname, scores, race claims, run tickets and feedback from the server. A one-way hash stays to reject late uploads. Your stamps, upgrades and local bests stay on this device.

This cannot be undone.
