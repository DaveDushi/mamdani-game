How to play

Change lanesFollow the cream chevrons through the gate. Use ← →, A / D, or swipe sideways. Only a gold ticket pays a stamp.

Jump a hurdleYellow ↑ means jump over the hurdle. Press ↑, W or Space, or swipe up.

Slide under a beamCyan ↓ means slide under the beam. Press ↓ or S, or swipe down.

One hit brings Trump onto the street. Take another hit within five seconds and he catches you. Hit a bottle and “Haram!” reverses left/right and jump/slide for five seconds.

The rainbow flag grants seven seconds of invincibility and pulls in stamps. A keffiyeh shields you for five seconds. A mask clears traffic for three. Chain three clean dodges, jumps or slides within five seconds of each other for one bonus stamp.

For every 100 stamps collected, City Hall takes 90 and you keep 10. The same rate applies to the rest at the finish. Even one stamp adds 0.1 to your wallet. Bank 150 to unlock Night Shift.

Family races. Reach 1,000 m to race Donald Jr. in a 100 m dash. Tap the sprint pad or press Space repeatedly. Each win sends you farther down the street toward the next rival. Losing ends the run and banks your stamps. Spend them on Race speed in Shop & outfits. Beat all five children and Donald to reach Overtime.
