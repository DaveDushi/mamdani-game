Follow the painted lane. Jump cabs and low hurdles. Slide under beams, scaffolds and food carts. Either move clears a ticket rail. The first gates have labels.

When a delivery dolly crosses, take the lane it leaves open. Jump a low signal boom or duck a high one. Jump the blue ice patch or take a dry lane. On ice, steering locks for half a second.

A bell and road marking announce a horse-drawn carriage. Leave its lane. At a protest, duck the police tape, then jump the fallen placard. Later pairs can switch lanes.

At the open Central Park dock, move left again from the left lane to take a bike. Steer through gaps to earn stamps. You can hop a low barrier. The path rejoins Fifth Avenue.

Without protection, hitting a STREET CLOSED wall head-on ends the run. Other traffic hits start a five-second chase. Hit traffic again during the chase and Trump catches you. Bottles reverse your controls for five seconds (“Haram!”).

The rainbow draws in stamps and makes you invincible for 7 seconds. A keffiyeh shields for 5 seconds. A mask clears traffic for 3 seconds. Steer the Fast & Free bus for 10 seconds. Smashing vehicles or worksite obstacles earns up to 3 stamps per ride.

Stamps. Grab tickets. Chain three clean moves less than 5 seconds apart for a bonus stamp. During a 16-second 78° Heatwave, that streak pays 2 stamps. Bank 10% at the finish. Night Shift unlocks at 150 lifetime banked stamps, including those spent on training.

Family sprints. Junior’s opening checkpoint is 650 m. On “Go!”, tap or press Space in a steady rhythm. Beat the five kids in order, even offline. Each one needs a faster pace. A loss ends the run.

Donald’s final. Win all five heats in one run, then go beyond both 9 km and the live season record while online. Race Donald for 100 m. Each win changes one sixth of the city. Beat Donald for 100% takeover and endless Overtime at 10% higher speed. Day Run and Night Shift share the 2.3.1 rankings.
